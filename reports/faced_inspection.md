## Update 2 after reading `datasets/FACED/Code/` (supersedes earlier statements)
- **Preprocessing actually applied (`Code/Preprocessing/main.py`):** unit fix to µV → **last 30 s before each clip's end trigger** (so the stored 30 s are the END of each clip) → resample 250 Hz → band-pass **0.05–47 Hz** → bad-channel interpolation (MNE) → **ICA removing ocular components** (Fp1/2 as EOG proxy) → **common-average reference over all 32 channels (including the two mastoid/HEOG channels)** → cohort-1 channels reordered. **No 50 Hz notch is applied**, so the "notch + 0.5 Hz" claim in the pasted text was wrong; the 50 Hz residual we measured is leakage past the 47 Hz low-pass transition plus any line noise. Consequences: our pipeline must still apply 4–45 Hz band-pass and 50 Hz notch; re-CAR over the 12 shared channels is valid (the data already carries a 32-ch CAR); sub023/sub030 etc. are artifacts that survived ICA.
- **Ratings (d) — now on disk and checked.** `After_remark[i]` has `score` (12 items), `trial` (presentation order), `vid` (video idx 1–28), `Accuracy`, `ResponseTime`. All 123 subjects × 28 videos × 12 items, `vid` is a permutation of 1–28 for every subject, 0 NaN, range 0–7, continuous (2,624 distinct valence values). Items order: joy, tenderness, inspiration, amusement, anger, disgust, fear, sadness, arousal, valence, familiarity, liking. Mean valence by category (anger…tenderness, neutral between): 1.70, 1.64, 1.57, 1.86 (neg), neutral 3.18, 4.23, 4.79, 4.37, 4.43 (pos) — sensible. Binarised at the midpoint 3.5: valence-high 43.3%, arousal-high 53.9%, 8 exact-3.5 ties. **There is no dominance item.** Ratings are NOT aligned in the pickles by anything but `vid`; sort each subject's `score` by `vid` to match pickle clip order (done in the check, to be coded in `io/faced.py`).
