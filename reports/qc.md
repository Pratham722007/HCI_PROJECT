## Domain-shift check

Logistic regression on 36 log band-power features (12 ch x theta/alpha/gamma) predicting the dataset of origin, balanced (<=4000 windows/dataset), subject-wise 5-fold CV. Chance = 0.333. **Accuracy = 0.651 +- 0.033** (folds: 0.664, 0.710, 0.631, 0.633, 0.620).
A value far above chance means the corpora are separable from band power alone (strong dataset shift); the paper must discuss this as a limitation of zero-shot cross-corpus transfer.
